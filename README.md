# GeneratingSurprises
Gift Bag Simulator – An Application for Personalized Surprises and Gifts

Gift Bag Simulator is a unique and interactive application designed to help users create and distribute personalized surprises with advanced functionalities and an intuitive command system. It brings joy and creativity to the process of gifting by combining various features and customization options.

Key Features:
  -Diverse Types of Surprises: Enjoy fortune cookies with inspirational quotes, delicious candies of various flavors, and adorable Minion toys with delightful personalities.
  -Multiple Delivery Methods: Surprises can be delivered with enthusiastic applause, warm hugs, or cheerful songs.
  -Advanced Bag System: Manage surprises using FIFO, LIFO, or RANDOM mechanisms, providing flexibility and creativity.
  -Intuitive Commands: A well-defined command system allows users to construct, add, remove, and give surprises efficiently.
  -Automatic Generation and Creative Distribution: Gifts are randomly generated and delivered with captivating flair, making every moment special.

How It Works:
  -Choose the desired bag type (FIFO, LIFO, or RANDOM) and set the wait time between deliveries.
  -Generate your favorite surprises (candies, fortune cookies, or Minion toys).
  -Use commands like construct, putBag, putSurprise, or takeOut to manage bags and surprises.
  -Deliver surprises individually or all at once, with passionate styles such as applause, songs, or hugs.
  -Experience the joy of giving and the delight of recipients.

Benefits:
  -Offers an engaging user experience through complex yet intuitive functionalities.
  -Ideal for organizing special events, creating heartfelt gifts for friends and family, or simply spreading unexpected smiles.
